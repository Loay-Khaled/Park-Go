The Urban Challenge & Solution

Rethinking Parking from Frustration to Seamless Precision

Urban drivers spend an average of 14 minutes circling for parking, causing up to 30% of inner-city traffic. Meanwhile, venue owners suffer from untracked cash leakages, low turnover, and blind spots.

✕

The Traditional Reality

  • Circling & Bottlenecks: Drivers block entrances while frantically searching for spots, degrading customer satisfaction.
  • Revenue Leakage & Overstays: Paper tickets get lost, cash handling is unverified, and overtime vehicles stay unpenalized.
  • Zero Operational Data: Facility operators have no live count of actual occupancy, peak hours, or customer frequency.
  • Accessibility Friction: Designated disability bays are routinely abused without digital verification or enforcement.
✓

The Park & Go Standard

  • Guaranteed Advance Spot: Drivers book their exact spot and zone before arriving, driving straight to an illuminated slot.
  • 100% Cashless & Contactless: Secure QR check-in/out, automated in-app digital wallet billing, and overstay calculation.
  • Cloud Executive Telemetry: Facility managers monitor real-time occupancy heatmaps, peak times, and revenue in one dashboard.
  • Automated Queue & Appeals: Smart waiting lists auto-offer freshly vacated spots with a 5-minute reserved window.

Operational Flow

Effortless for Drivers. Completely Automated for Owners.

From the moment a customer opens the app to their frictionless departure, Park & Go eliminates manual queues and optimizes every minute of parking turnover.

01

Discover & Select

Driver checks live bay availability, filters for accessibility or preferred floors, and books their guaranteed spot before leaving home.

02

Arrive & Scan QR

Driver approaches the entry barrier. The camera scans their unique in-app dynamic QR code, lifting the gate in under a second.

03

Seamless Parking

Turn-by-turn guidance leads the driver straight to their reserved bay. Live timers monitor duration and send friendly expiration reminders.

04

Automated Cashless Exit

At departure, the exit scanner validates the pass. The system computes exact duration, bills the in-app digital wallet, and releases the spot.

What the Location Owner Receives:

Zero manual ticket handling, automated tariff calculation (including grace periods and overstay penalties), comprehensive audit trails, and live multi-zone occupancy control.

Our Journey

Born from Academic Rigor, Scaled for Enterprise Mobility

We founded Park & Go to solve real-world parking chaos with elegant technology and reliable engineering.

Park & Go originated as an intensive engineering pilot at a premier university campus. Faced with severe morning gridlocks, hundreds of student vehicles searching for parking simultaneously, and inefficient manual ticketing, our team engineered an end-to-end IoT and cloud solution.

The pilot proved that with real-time zone telemetry, guaranteed slot bookings, and instant QR entry, parking transit time plummeted by over 65%, while spot utilization reached peak efficiency.

Today, that proven architecture has been scaled into Park & Go — a multi-facility enterprise platform ready to power shopping malls, commercial business districts, universities, airports, and private garage operators across the region.

🎯 Our Mission

To eliminate parking friction and vehicle idling by turning every physical parking bay into an intelligent, connected digital asset.

🚀 Our Vision

To become the region’s premier urban mobility network, enabling drivers to reach any destination with guaranteed access and zero wasted minutes.

🛡️ Our Core Values

Speed, operational transparency, rock-solid uptime, and genuine partner revenue growth.
